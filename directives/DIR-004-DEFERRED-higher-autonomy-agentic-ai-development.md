> DEFERRED: This directive is not part of the initial AI code-generation governance release and grants no permission. Higher-autonomy and agentic software-development capabilities require separate approval until this directive is completed and activated.

## Purpose

Define future controls for AI software-development capabilities that operate with greater autonomy than approved developer-in-the-loop workflows.

## Scope

Potential higher-autonomy capabilities include AI systems that can independently inspect or modify multiple files, execute engineering commands, iterate on failures, or create repository changes without human approval at each step.
