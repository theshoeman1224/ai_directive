## Objective

Maintain a public, durable planning and governance record that can be reviewed by humans and safely used by AI assistants. The specific program objective is TBD.

## Scope

This plan covers planning, decisions, directives, final significant communications, and supporting notes that are safe for public release.

It excludes implementation software, operational systems, private source material, and sensitive organizational details.

## Current priorities

* Complete human review of the initial repository structure and safeguards.
* Assign an owner and determine whether this plan is ready for approval.
* Replace this starter content with sanitized, program-specific planning content when available.

## Requirements

* Keep GitHub as the durable source of truth.
* Preserve rationale, approval state, traceability, and historical context.
* Make active instructions unmistakable.
* Sanitize every contribution for a public repository.
* Keep governance lightweight and readable without custom tooling.

## Operating model

Meaningful changes normally move through a branch, pull request, review, explicit metadata update, and merge. Discussion and notes inform changes but do not approve them. Approved decisions preserve rationale, active directives govern worker action, and final communications preserve issued wording.

## Implementation plan

### Phase 1

### Phase 2

### Phase 3

Maintain the plan and current-state briefing as work proceeds. Record durable rationale in decisions, issue worker instructions through directives, and preserve final significant communications.
