## Context

A fictional team uses generated text in public documents and needs a clear review point before publication.

## Decision

In this fictional example, a designated reviewer would inspect generated documents for accuracy and public-release safety before publication.

## Rationale

Generated text may contain factual errors or repeat private prompt context. A human review would provide a clear release check.

## Alternatives considered

* Publish generated text without review. Rejected in the example because errors and private details could be released.
* Prohibit generated text. Rejected in the example because review addresses the stated risk with less restriction.

## Consequences

* Expected benefit: fewer factual and public-data errors.
* Tradeoff: publication takes additional review time.
* Risk: reviewers may treat the check as automatic rather than inspect the content.
* Operational impact: the fictional workflow gains one review step.
